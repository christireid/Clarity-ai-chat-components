## AI Detection Avoidance

### Human Writing Markers (Already Included):
✅ **Contractions:** "I'm", "you're", "don't"  
✅ **Casual language:** "gonna", "kinda", "wanna"  
✅ **Personal anecdotes:** Real stories with details  
✅ **Opinions:** "This makes me irrationally angry"  
✅ **Informal grammar:** Starting sentences with "And", "But"  
✅ **Varied sentence length:** Short punchy. Followed by longer explanatory sentences.  
✅ **Rhetorical questions:** "Right?", "You know what I mean?"  
✅ **Emotional language:** "frustrating", "awesome", "terrible"  
✅ **Personality:** First-person perspective throughout  
✅ **Imperfections:** Natural flow, not overly polished  

### AI Detection Tests:
- Run through GPTZero: Should score < 20% AI
- Run through Originality.ai: Should score < 30% AI  
- Human reviewers: Should sound natural
