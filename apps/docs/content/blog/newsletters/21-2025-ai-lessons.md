# Newsletter: What We Learned Building AI Chat in 2025

**Subject:** 3 years of AI chat development, distilled

---

We've been building AI chat interfaces since GPT-3. Here's what we've learned.

## The Lessons

**1. Users don't want AI. They want solutions.**

Nobody wakes up thinking "I'd love to chat with an LLM today." They want their code reviewed, their question answered, their problem solved.

The AI is invisible in a good interface. The solution is visible.

**2. Speed perception > actual speed**

A 2-second response with good loading states feels faster than a 1.5-second response with a spinner.

Humans don't measure milliseconds. They measure anxiety.

**3. Errors are conversation opportunities**

The worst thing you can do: `Something went wrong. Please try again.`

The best thing: "I couldn't complete that request because [reason]. Would you like me to [alternative]?"

**4. Context management is everything**

The difference between "helpful AI" and "frustrating AI" is usually context. Did the AI remember what you said? Does it understand what you're working on?

LLMs have amnesia. Your app needs memory.

**5. Cost optimization is a feature**

Not for you—for your users. The difference between "$50/month AI usage" and "$5/month" is the difference between "cool experiment" and "indispensable tool."
