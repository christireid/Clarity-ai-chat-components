## Overview

**Total Posts:** 24

**Duration:** 12 weeks (3 months)

## Strategic Sequencing Rationale

1. **Weeks 1-2:** Hook with high-value, accessible content
2. **Weeks 3-4:** Build credibility with production patterns
3. **Weeks 5-6:** Address cost concerns (budget season)
4. **Weeks 7-8:** Advanced patterns for engaged readers
5. **Weeks 9-10:** Security and compliance topics
6. **Weeks 11-12:** Thought leadership and wrap-up
