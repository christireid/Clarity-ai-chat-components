### Lessons Learned
- Staggered animations add polish
- Layered shadows create depth
- Spring physics feel natural
- Rounded-2xl more modern

### Patterns Established
- Use cubic-bezier [0.25, 0.1, 0.25, 1]
- Stagger delays by 0.05s per item
- Shadow system: 0_2px_8px for cards
- Border opacity: /40 default, /60 hover
- Rounded-2xl for cards, rounded-xl for buttons
