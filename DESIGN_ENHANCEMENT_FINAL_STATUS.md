## Design System Standards

### Shadow System
All components now use specific rgba shadow values:
- **Small elements**: `shadow-[0_1px_2px_rgba(15,23,42,0.08)]`
- **Default**: `shadow-[0_1px_3px_rgba(15,23,42,0.1)]`
- **Hover**: `shadow-[0_4px_12px_rgba(15,23,42,0.15)]`
- **Card hover**: `shadow-[0_10px_24px_rgba(15,23,42,0.12)]`
- **Elevated surfaces**: `shadow-[0_24px_48px_rgba(15,23,42,0.32)]`
- **Tooltip**: `shadow-[0_16px_32px_rgba(15,23,42,0.32)]`

### Border System
- **Consistent opacity**: `border-border/60` or `border-2 border-border/60`
- **Better visual hierarchy**: Improved contrast and depth

### Focus System
- **Focus rings**: `focus-visible:ring-ring/50 focus-visible:ring-[3px]`
- **Enhanced accessibility**: Better keyboard navigation visibility

### Backdrop Blur
- **Overlays**: `backdrop-blur-sm` or `backdrop-blur-md`
- **Modals**: Enhanced depth perception

### Transitions
- **Consistent timing**: `transition-all duration-200` or `transition-colors duration-300`
- **Polished interactions**: Smooth hover and focus states
