## Market Landscape

### Industry Overview

The AI component library market has exploded in 2025-2026 with **24+ significant entrants**, ranging
from copy-paste component collections (shadcn/ui AI) to full-stack SDK frameworks (Vercel AI SDK,
LangChain). The market is segmented across three distinct categories:

1. **Pure UI Libraries** (shadcn/ui AI, Prompt Kit, Magic UI)
   - Focus: Beautiful, copy-paste components
   - Strength: Design quality, customization
   - Weakness: No backend integration, no cost management

2. **SDK Frameworks** (Vercel AI SDK, LangChain, CopilotKit)
   - Focus: Backend integration, state management
   - Strength: Multi-provider support, streaming hooks
   - Weakness: UI components secondary or missing

3. **Integrated Solutions** (Ant Design X, Assistant UI, **Clarity Chat**)
   - Focus: Complete component + logic packages
   - Strength: Faster time-to-production
   - Weakness: Less flexible than SDK frameworks
