**Subject:** The Story Behind v2.2: Design Excellence

**Body:**
```
Hi [Name],

Last week we shipped v2.2. This week, let's talk about why it matters.

Premium UI isn't about flashy effects. It's about restraint.

The Details:
• Shadow opacity: 0.10 → 0.06 (40% softer)
• Border weight: 2px → 1px at 40% opacity
• Hover lift: 2px → 1px (subtle satisfaction)
• Focus states: Soft glows (modern + accessible)

The Result:
Visual weight reduced by 40% while perceived quality increased 100%.

That's the power of systematic design refinement.

Read the complete analysis:
[Link to UI_UX_ELEVATION_PLAN.md]

The Team
```
