## 🎨 **Visual Examples**

### Button - Before vs After

**Before (v2.1):**
- Shadow: `0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.05)` - Visible
- Hover: `-translate-y-0.5` (2px lift) - Noticeable
- Focus: `ring-2 ring-ring` - Hard outline
- Border (outline): `border-2` (2px) - Heavy

**After (v2.2):**
- Shadow: `0 1px 2px rgba(0,0,0,0.04)` - Whisper-soft ✨
- Hover: `-translate-y-px` (1px lift) - Subtle ✨
- Focus: `ring-1 + shadow-focus-primary` - Soft glow ✨
- Border (outline): `border` (1px at 40%) - Light ✨

## 💡 **Key Concepts**

### The "Whisper-Soft" Philosophy
Premium interfaces don't demand attention - they guide it softly.

### The 40% Rule
Reduce visual weight by ~40%:
- Shadows: 40% softer
- Borders: 40% opacity (default)
- Hover backgrounds: 40-50% opacity

### The 1px Standard
- Borders: 1px (not 2px)
- Hover lift: 1px (not 2px)
- Focus ring: 1px (not 2px)

### The Soft Glow Approach
Modern focus states glow instead of outlining.
